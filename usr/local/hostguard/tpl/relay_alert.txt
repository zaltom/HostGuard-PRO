This is usually the first visible sign of a compromised site. The
account is real and the credentials are real, so nothing in the firewall
or the authentication logs looks wrong; the volume is the only symptom.

If the account is not expected to send at this rate, look in the script
directory above for what is sending on its behalf. A form without a
captcha and a contact page that does not validate its recipient are the
two usual causes.
