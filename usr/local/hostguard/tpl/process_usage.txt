The process had been running longer than PROC_USAGE_TIME before it was
considered, so this is sustained usage rather than a startup spike.

HostGuard Pro sends SIGTERM at most, never SIGKILL. A process that
ignores SIGTERM is left alone deliberately: deciding to kill it outright
is a judgement about what it was doing, which a monitor cannot make.
