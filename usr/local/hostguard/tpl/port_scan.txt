HostGuard Pro - Port Scan Detected
==================================

Server:   [HOSTNAME]
Time:     [TIME]

Address:  [IP]

This address connected to more closed ports than SCAN_LIMIT allows
within SCAN_INTERVAL, so the kernel added it to the temporary block
set. The block expires on its own.

Only traffic that matched no open port is counted, so ordinary clients
do not trigger this. A single scan is background noise on any public
address; a scan followed by authentication failures is worth reading
together.

To remove the block:
  hostguard -tr [IP]
