HostGuard Pro - Excessive Logins
==================================

Server:   [HOSTNAME]
Time:     [TIME]

Address:  [IP]
Logins:   [COUNT] in the current hour
Limit:    [LIMIT]
Accounts: [USERS]

[ACTION]

An address authenticating this often is usually a mail client polling
far too frequently. It can also be someone working through a list of
stolen credentials, which looks identical from the logs except that the
account names vary.

If the accounts above are all the same, suspect a misconfigured client.
If they vary, suspect credential testing.
