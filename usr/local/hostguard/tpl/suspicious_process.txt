Nothing has been terminated. This check only ever reports, because none
of the things it looks for is proof on its own: an administrator may
well run a listener or an interpreter one-liner on purpose.

What is worth checking, in order: whether the account should be running
anything at all, whether the binary path makes sense, and what the
process has open (lsof -p [PID]).

If it is expected, add an exception to:
  /etc/hostguard/procignore.conf
